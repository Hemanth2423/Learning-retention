# Prompt log: Learning science + V2 plan

## The prompt

> Now, let's focus on building the best possible app for learning itself. Read through these links and understand the core neuroscience behind learning. [...] get the core philosophy and extract information required to build an awesome learning app and store it in a md file. This can act as the scientific philosophy.

Six sources: Squire & Dede 2015, Kandel 2001, Rasch & Born 2013, Smolen et al. 2016, a Psychology Today piece on desirable difficulties, and Dunlosky et al. 2013.

The rethought journey I asked for: learn a concept, from a book or from scratch, at my existing level, high level first and then the why, keep checking progress, and store key information in a knowledge base. The philosophy: a few simple steps to start, gamified like Codecademy, and lots of varied questions with active thinking.
